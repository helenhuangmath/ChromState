
Current problem: 
Epigenetics researchers are always wondering what are the chromatin states and dynamics of one state to another, what are the bivalent or poised states. There states or epigenetic features are important for downstream treatment. For example, the epigenetic drug can go into the core bivalent features or TFs and tune the whole genome to another states, so that cancers or chronic diseases or metabolic related states could change to other treatable states. 

However, there are lacking quantitative methods to quantify chromatin states. Current tool ChromHMM is only a qualitative description, and sometimes confusing, it is hard to trace back which specific state or region is important and reversible. So I want to create a package which can quantify chromatin states, a upgraded version of ChromHMM. 

I want to create this ChromState package, help me to make a plan of how to do it
